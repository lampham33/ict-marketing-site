Who is the Inner Circle Trader?

“Inner Circle Trader,” shortened to ICT, is the online name of Michael J. Huddleston, a trader who shares a large body of free teaching on price action. His ideas cover candlestick behavior, liquidity, fair value gaps, order blocks, and trading at set times of day. Many students and many content creators now teach their own versions, so this site collects the main ideas in one organized place.

What is market liquidity?

Liquidity is where many orders are waiting, such as stop-losses sitting above a recent high or below a recent low. In ICT teaching, price often travels toward these pools of orders before it reverses or continues. Learning to spot them is the starting point for everything else on this site.
