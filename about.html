About this site

We are an inner circle trader education site built as a class project for MKTG:4400 at the University of Iowa. Our mission is to teach retail futures and forex traders how to read market structure, order flow, fair value, and liquidity zones, and to show when session kill zones bring the most activity.

Why we built it

Searching for ICT topics returns plenty of material, mostly from niche trading blogs, creator videos, and mid-sized education sites. That material is scattered and often hard to follow. We organize the same topics into one clear path, with diagrams and short step lists, so a beginner can move from one idea to the next.

Who it is for

Students and newcomers with no trading background. We define each term, explain it in small steps, and link every lesson to the next one.

What we value

Clarity

Short sections and simple words, with each page doing one job.

Honesty

We state clearly that trading is risky and that no setup is guaranteed.

Access

Every lesson is free, and the layout works on phones and desktops.

Important reminders

This site is fictional. It sells nothing, collects no personal information, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any trader, brand, or company. It gives no financial advice.
